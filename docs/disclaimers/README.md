# OurArchive Community Disclaimers & Governance Charter

> **Emergency Community Notice & Platform Scope Disclaimer**  
> **OurArchive** is an emergency, temporary measure to aid Archive of Our Own (AO3) users during service outages and is strictly not intended to replace, compete with, or replicate the permanent operations of Archive of Our Own (AO3) or the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW).

---

## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

OurArchive was created in response to unexpected and extended downtime of the Archive of Our Own (AO3). In moments when fans, readers, and creators cannot access their community library, OurArchive provides an emergency public bridge where users can share and read view-only Google Drive backups of fan works.

To protect the integrity of the fanfiction community, the platform adheres strictly to four legal, ethical, and operational pillars:
1. **Respect for Creator Sovereignty**: Fanfic authors retain 100% ownership and control over their works.
2. **Drive Owner Transparency**: Google Drive owners who share view-only links are protected with clear privacy and masking options.
3. **Solidarity with AO3 / OTW**: The service is explicitly non-commercial, non-replacement, and temporary.
4. **Immediate, Unconditional Takedown**: Any author or link owner who requests removal will have their content removed immediately without delay or contest.

## 3. General Principles

### Non-Commercial Nature
OurArchive does not run advertisements, does not solicit donations, does not sell user data, and does not charge for access. All infrastructure is deployed on free/community-tier services (Vercel and Supabase) purely for the benefit of readers and authors.

### Zero-Download Architecture
OurArchive is an index and reader proxy, not a file storage host. The platform never downloads, duplicates, or permanently stores Google Drive files on its servers. When a user reads a work, the text is rendered ephemerally in a distraction-free, read-only reader view.

### Fast-Track Resolution
If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding a link listed on OurArchive, please refer directly to the [Immediate Takedown & Removal Request Guide](takedown_and_removal_request.md).
