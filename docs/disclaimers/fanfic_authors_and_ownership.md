# Fanfiction Authors & Intellectual Property Rights

> **Emergency Community Notice & Platform Scope Disclaimer**  
> **OurArchive** is an emergency, temporary measure to aid fanfiction readers and authors during service outages on primary fanwork archives, and is strictly not intended to replace, compete with, or replicate the permanent operations of existing community fanfiction platforms.

---

## 1. Absolute Creator Ownership

All fanfiction stories, original characters, transformative narratives, and associated creative writing indexed on OurArchive belong **100% to their respective original authors**. 

OurArchive:
- **Claims zero intellectual property rights**, copyright, or proprietary interest in any indexed work.
- **Does not license, sub-license, or commercialize** any fan work.
- **Does not modify, edit, or monetize** any author's writing.
- Acts solely as a transient, emergency directory for view-only community links during primary archive service disruptions.

---

## 2. Attribution & Credit

Every search result and reader view within OurArchive makes every reasonable technical effort to display author attribution:
- Author Name / Pseudonym (as extracted from document metadata or Google Drive titles).
- Link back to the author's original profile or preferred social channel (where provided in the work).
- Distinction between the **Original Author** and the **Community Contributor / Submitter** who shared the backup link.

---

## 3. Author Consent & Fast-Track Opt-Out

We recognize that some authors prefer their works not to be mirrored or indexed outside of their official archive account, even during service emergencies. **We fully respect and honor this preference without question.**

If you are an author and find your work indexed on OurArchive:
1. You do not need to prove damages, fill out cumbersome DMCA paperwork, or wait for lengthy review cycles.
2. Simply send an email or message containing your work's title or the link (see [Takedown & Removal Request](takedown_and_removal_request.md)).
3. The administrator will remove the link and all associated metadata immediately.

---

## 4. Preservation of Author Disclaimers

Any content warnings, archive warnings, relationship tags, author's notes, and original disclaimers embedded within the original work's text are preserved intact in the view-only reader. OurArchive does not strip, alter, or censor an author's stated warnings.
