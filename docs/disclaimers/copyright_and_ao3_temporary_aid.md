# Copyright, Fair Use & AO3 Solidarity

> **Emergency Community Notice & Platform Scope Disclaimer**  
> **OurArchive** is an emergency, temporary measure to aid Archive of Our Own (AO3) users during service outages and is strictly not intended to replace, compete with, or replicate the permanent operations of Archive of Our Own (AO3) or the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW).

---

## 1. Solidarity with Archive of Our Own (AO3) & OTW

Archive of Our Own (AO3), operated by the non-profit Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), is the premier, gold-standard archive for transformative fan fiction, culture, and preservation.

**OurArchive stands in total solidarity with AO3, the OTW, and the global fan community.**
- OurArchive does **NOT** seek to replace AO3.
- OurArchive does **NOT** compete with AO3.
- OurArchive does **NOT** duplicate AO3's permanent archiving mission or infrastructure.

OurArchive exists exclusively as a transient, emergency community bridge during unexpected periods of AO3 infrastructure downtime or extended maintenance. Once AO3 returns to stable, accessible operational status, emergency link ingestion into OurArchive will be sunsetted.

---

## 2. Transformative Works & Fair Use

Transformative fanfiction has long been recognized as fair use under copyright law:
1. **Non-Commercial Nature**: OurArchive is completely non-commercial. It earns no ad revenue, charges no subscription fees, accepts no donations, and operates on free-tier community infrastructure.
2. **Transformative Culture**: The works indexed are non-profit transformative creative expressions celebrating source materials.
3. **Emergency Preservation**: Providing temporary, read-only public access to community backups during an outage serves a purely educational, social, and preservation-oriented purpose without harming the market for any original or commercial work.

---

## 3. Zero-Download Read-Only Compliance

To ensure compliance with copyright ethics and prevent digital piracy:
- **No File Hosting**: OurArchive does not maintain copies of `.pdf`, `.epub`, or `.docx` files on its servers.
- **Index Only**: The database only stores URL references pointing to user-managed Google Drive files.
- **Read-Only Rendering**: Readers view text dynamically streamed from the authorized view-only Google Drive link.
- **Ban on Commercial / Non-Fanfic Media**: The platform strictly prohibits commercial pirated material (e.g. copyrighted commercial e-books, movie rips, commercial audio files, software binaries). Any non-fanfic or commercial uploads will be instantly rejected by automated MIME filters and administrators.

---

## 4. Community Good Faith & Takedown Commitment

If any copyright holder, author, or organization believes that an indexed link infringes upon their rights, OurArchive maintains an **unconditional immediate takedown commitment**:
- We do not contest takedown requests.
- We do not impose legalistic burdens or delays.
- Upon receiving a request, the link and metadata will be permanently expunged immediately.

For instructions, see the [Immediate Takedown & Removal Request Guide](takedown_and_removal_request.md).
